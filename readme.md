## 📖 Overview

**Femora** is an open, modern, and privacy-conscious MERN stack web application engineered to bridge the critical gap in women's health awareness and preventive wellness. From adolescent menstrual education to maternal care, mental well-being, and nutritional guidance, Femora centralizes verified health resources and intelligent tracking tools into a unified, easy-to-use digital platform.

---

## ⚠️ Problem Statement & Impact

In many communities—especially across developing regions—women's health remains heavily stigmatized, under-resourced, and poorly understood. Adolescents and adult women frequently struggle to access reliable, culturally sensitive, and medically verified guidance.

### Who is Affected?
* **Teen Girls & Adolescents:** Navigating pubertal transitions and menstrual cycles without structured guidance.
* **Adult Women:** Managing hormonal balance, reproductive choices, career-life stress, and wellness.
* **Pregnant Women & New Mothers:** Requiring trimester-wise maternal care, postpartum support, and infant wellness data.
* **Caregivers & Families:** Looking to offer informed support to daughters, partners, and mothers.
* **Healthcare Educators:** Seeking a clean, reliable channel to disseminate verified medical advice.

### Current Challenges & Shortcomings of Existing Solutions
| Existing Market Deficiencies | Femora's Solution |
|:---|:---|
| **Fragmented Information:** Health advice is scattered across blogs, ad-heavy forums, and social media. | **Unified Hub:** A single verified repository covering menstruation, maternal care, mental health, and fitness. |
| **Complex Jargon:** Scientific data is often written in intimidating clinical terminology. | **Plain-Language Medical Content:** Medically reviewed guides written for everyday comprehension. |
| **Stigma & Privacy Barriers:** Taboos deter women from visiting clinics for basic queries. | **Safe, Private Digital Space:** Anonymous browsing, discreet tracking, and confidential data storage. |
| **Single-Focus Apps:** Most tools only offer a calendar or a blog, failing to provide holistic care. | **Comprehensive Suite:** Cycle tracker, wellness blogs, clinic locator, and emergency hotlines in one app. |
